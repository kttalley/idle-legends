Stillmarch — Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 September 2026

We do not ask who you are. Stillmarch has no sign-up form. There is no name, no email address, no password and no social login. You can play the entire game without providing a single piece of personal information.

What is stored, and why

  • An anonymous account identifier. The first time the game contacts our server it creates an anonymous account — a random identifier with no personal details attached. It exists so your cloud save and your leaderboard position belong to you.
  • Your character and its progress. Character name, class, race, faction, level, equipment and progress, so the game can back up your save and place you on the leaderboard. Your character name is visible to other players. Choose it accordingly.
  • Duel records. Who fought whom and the outcome.
  • Reports and blocks. If you report or block a player we store who was reported, the name shown at the time, and the reason, so we can act on it. If you are reported, the same is stored about you.
  • Purchase state. Whether you own each in-app purchase.

What is not stored

  • No advertising identifiers. The game contains no advertising and no ad SDK.
  • No analytics or behavioural tracking.
  • No contacts, photos, location or health data. The game never asks for them.
  • No chat content. Conversation in the game is generated on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

Who else is involved

  • Supabase hosts the database holding the items listed above.
  • RevenueCat records which in-app purchases you own, against an anonymous identifier, so purchases can be restored on a new device.
  • Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) processes payments, depending on where you installed the game. We never receive your payment details.

Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with anyone else.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules them locally on your device to tell you when your hero’s watch is ending. They are not sent from a server and involve no data leaving your device.

Children

Stillmarch is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. To delete your server-side data — your anonymous account, cloud saves and leaderboard entry — email talleykristian@gmail.com. Records relating to abuse reports may be retained where needed to keep other players safe.
