Privacy Policy

Stillmarch · last updated 11 October 2026

Stillmarch is a game made by an independent developer. It is designed to work offline and to need as little information about you as possible. There is no sign-up form, no advertising, no advertising or third-party analytics SDK, and no tracking across other apps or websites.

What the app collects

DataWhyWho processes it
An anonymous user ID Created automatically the first time the game reaches our server: a random identifier with no personal details attached. It ties your cloud save, ladder entry and the other items below to you. Supabase (our database provider)
Your characters and their progress Backed up to our server so they survive a reinstall, and used for the optional ladder. This includes the character name you chose, which other players can see. Supabase
Progress milestones The first time each character reaches a milestone — its first kill, certain levels, its first quest, returning a day or a week later — we record which milestone, when, how long you had played, and whether you are on iOS or Android. Used only to see where new players get stuck so we can improve the game. Supabase
Duel records Who fought whom on the ladder, and the outcome. Supabase
Reports and blocks If you report or block a player we store who was reported, the name shown at the time, and the reason, so we can act on it. If you are reported, the same is stored about you. Supabase
Purchase history To deliver and restore in-app purchases you have made, and to know which items your account owns. Apple or Google, and RevenueCat (our purchase infrastructure provider)

These are linked to your anonymous ID within the game. None of them are used to track you across other apps or websites, and none are sold or shared with advertisers or data brokers.

What the app does not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or postal address.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA or Android advertising ID), and no cross-app or cross-site tracking.
  • No third-party analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • No chat content. Conversation in the game is generated on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

Your game data

Your characters, progress and settings live on your device, and a copy is backed up to our server against your anonymous ID so a reinstall does not lose them. There is no account to create and no login to play.

The optional ladder

If you use the ladder, a snapshot of your character — its name, level, class, race, equipment and statistics — is published so other players can see and challenge it. It contains no personal information about you. Other characters shown walking the world are computer-controlled, not real people.

Payments

Purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), depending on where you installed the game. We never see or receive your payment details. RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt so the app can confirm what you own and restore it on a new device.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules them locally on your device to tell you when your hero’s watch is ending. They are not sent from a server.

Children

Stillmarch is rated 9+ and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights and deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. To delete your server-side data — your anonymous account, cloud saves, ladder entry and progress milestones — email the address below and it will be deleted. Records relating to abuse reports may be retained where needed to keep other players safe.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
