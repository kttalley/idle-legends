Stillmarch 1.2

• Sound effects! Swords, spells, hits, coins and roaring bosses, recorded fantasy sounds given a retro 16-bit grain to match the pixel art. Each spell school has its own sound.
• Quiet ambience for every region: birdsong in the meadows, a river at the crossing, wind on the peaks, crickets after dark and frogs in the swamps, with rain when the weather turns.
• The guitar theme now plays continuously, with no gap between loops, and the old background static and synth drones are gone.
• Separate Music, Sound effects and Ambience switches in Menu → Settings.
• Behind the scenes: we now record anonymous progress milestones (first kill, levels reached, coming back the next day) to find where new players get stuck. No personal information is collected; see the updated privacy policy.
