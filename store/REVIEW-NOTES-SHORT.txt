GUIDELINE 1.2 — USER-GENERATED CONTENT
The user-generated content in this app is the CHARACTER NAME, shown on the leaderboard and
above other players in the world. Zone chat, party chat and whispers are generated on the
device; no text a player types is ever sent to another player.

1. EULA — launch the app after installing. The Terms of Use screen is the FIRST screen,
   before character selection and before any account exists. It cannot be dismissed; the
   only way past is "I agree". Its first clause, visible without scrolling, states there is
   zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users.
   (There is no login or registration. The account is anonymous and is created silently the
   first time the leaderboard is contacted, so this is the earliest possible moment.)
   Full terms: https://design.kristiantalley.com/stillmarch/terms/

2. FILTER — on character creation, tap the quill icon by the hero name and type a slur or
   sexual term. The name is rejected on screen and "Begin Your Legend" is disabled until it
   changes. The filter normalises leet substitution, letter spacing, repeated letters and
   Cyrillic/Greek lookalikes. Names published before this build are masked on display.

3. FLAG — open LADDER (tap MENU in the bottom dock, then LADDER at the top of the panel).
   Every other player's row has a flag control on the right. It opens a report dialog with
   five reasons: hate speech or a slur, sexual or explicit content, harassment or threats,
   impersonation, and something else objectionable.

4. BLOCK — the same dialog offers "Block and report". Blocking removes that player from the
   leaderboard immediately on the same tap with no refresh, removes them from the player's
   world, notifies us by filing a report automatically, and persists across reinstalls and
   devices because it is stored server-side.

5. 24 HOURS — we review every report within 24 hours, remove the offending content and
   eject the account responsible. Ejection blanks the published name and bars that account
   from publishing again, enforced by database policy rather than by the app, so a modified
   client cannot bypass it. Abuse may also be reported to talleykristian@gmail.com, which is
   printed in the terms and on the terms screen.

GUIDELINE 2.1(b) — IN-APP PURCHASES
Both products are now on one SHOP screen reachable from anywhere: tap MENU in the bottom
dock, then the hamburger control, then SHOP (the first item in the menu). It lists Founder
Pack (com.stillmarch.game.founder) and Standing Orders
(com.stillmarch.game.standing_orders), and Restore Purchases, which is reachable without
making a purchase first.

The previous build fetched prices only through a RevenueCat "Offering", which is external
dashboard configuration; when that returned nothing the app showed only "Store unavailable"
and the Standing Orders control was hidden entirely. This build also requests the product
identifiers directly from StoreKit, can complete a purchase by that path, states the
specific reason when a price cannot load, and offers a Retry. A product row is now always
shown even when its price is unavailable. We have confirmed the Paid Apps Agreement is in
effect.

GENERAL
No credentials are needed; there is no account system and no demo account. Combat resolves
automatically. MENU opens the character sheet, talents, zone travel and The Watch; GEAR
opens equipment; CHAT opens the on-device message feed; LADDER opens the leaderboard.
Services: RevenueCat (purchases) and Supabase (leaderboard, blocks, reports). No ads, no
analytics, no IDFA. No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos are requested. All
artwork, music, text and code are original and owned by the developer.
