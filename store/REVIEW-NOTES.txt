STILLMARCH — review information

Submitted in response to submission ID ceda5a4d-d43b-41c6-a3e7-49f658564d2e (1.0 build 3),
which was rejected under guidelines 2.3.6, 1.2 and 2.1(b). All three are addressed in this
build. Sections 1 and 2 below are the walkthrough Apple asked to have in this field.

1. USER-GENERATED CONTENT — WHERE THE REQUIRED MECHANISMS ARE (guideline 1.2)

The user-generated content in this app is the CHARACTER NAME. It is published to the
leaderboard and appears above other players' characters in the world. Zone chat, party chat
and whispers are generated on the device by the app itself — no text a player types is ever
transmitted to another player.

  a) EULA, PRESENTED BEFORE ANY ACCOUNT EXISTS
     Launch the app after installing. The Terms of Use screen is the FIRST screen, ahead of
     character selection. It cannot be dismissed or skipped; the only way past it is the
     "I agree" button. The first clause on screen, without scrolling, states that there is
     zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users.
     (The app has no login or registration of any kind. The account is anonymous and is
     created silently the first time the leaderboard is contacted, so this screen is the
     earliest possible moment.)

  b) FILTERING OBJECTIONABLE CONTENT
     On the character creation screen, tap the quill icon beside the hero's name and type a
     slur or sexual term. The name is rejected on screen and the "Begin Your Legend" button
     is disabled until it is changed. The filter also normalises common evasions — leet
     substitution, letter spacing, repeated letters, and Cyrillic/Greek lookalike
     characters. Names published before this build are additionally masked on display.

  c) FLAGGING OBJECTIONABLE CONTENT
     Open LADDER (top of the left panel; on iPhone, tap MENU in the bottom dock first).
     Every other player's row carries a flag control (⚑) on the right. Tapping it opens a
     report dialog with five reasons: hate speech or a slur, sexual or explicit content,
     harassment or threats, impersonation, and something else objectionable.

  d) BLOCKING AN ABUSIVE USER
     The same dialog offers "Block and report". Tapping it:
       - removes that player from your leaderboard immediately, on the same tap, with no
         refresh and no network round trip;
       - removes them from your game world, so their character stops appearing;
       - notifies us, by filing a report automatically alongside the block;
       - persists across reinstalls and devices, as the block is stored server-side.

  e) ACTING WITHIN 24 HOURS
     We review every report within 24 hours, remove the offending content and eject the
     account responsible. Ejection blanks the published name and bars that account from
     publishing again; this is enforced by database policy, not by the app, so a modified
     client cannot bypass it. Abuse can also be reported directly to
     talleykristian@gmail.com, which is printed in the terms and on the terms screen.

2. IN-APP PURCHASES — WHERE THEY ARE (guideline 2.1(b))

Both products are now on a single SHOP screen, reachable from anywhere in the game:
tap MENU in the bottom dock, then the hamburger (☰) control, then SHOP — it is the first
item in the menu.

  - Founder Pack (USD 4.99, com.stillmarch.game.founder): three cosmetic colour schemes.
    Purely visual; no stats, speed or drop-rate change.
  - Standing Orders (USD 2.99, com.stillmarch.game.standing_orders): permanently extends
    unattended play by 8 hours. One time, not a refill, not a currency.
  - Restore Purchases is on the same screen and is reachable without making a purchase.

The previous build failed here because the app fetched prices only through a RevenueCat
"Offering", which is external dashboard configuration; when that lookup returned nothing,
the app showed only "Store unavailable" and the Standing Orders control was hidden entirely.
This build requests the product identifiers directly from StoreKit as well, can complete a
purchase by that path, states the specific reason when a price cannot be loaded, and offers
a Retry. A product row is now always displayed even when its price is unavailable.

3. WHAT THE APP IS, AND FOR WHOM

Stillmarch is an idle role-playing game. You create a hero, choose a class and spend talent
points; combat then resolves automatically while you watch, and continues while the app is
closed. The player's decisions are the build, the equipment, and which zone and fights to
take, rather than moment-to-moment input. It is for players who enjoy RPG progression but
cannot commit long sessions.

4. SETTING UP AND REACHING EVERY FEATURE

No credentials are required. There is no demo account because the app has no account system
and no login.
  - Launch, agree to the Terms of Use, pick a class and race, tap Begin. Play starts at once.
  - Combat runs by itself. The bottom bar switches between Fight, Mining and Smithing.
  - MENU (bottom dock) opens the left panel: character sheet, talent trees, zone travel, and
    The Watch (the offline-progress timer).
  - GEAR (bottom dock) opens equipment and inventory.
  - CHAT (bottom dock) opens the message feed. These messages are generated on the device
    and are not sent between players.
  - LADDER (top of the left panel) opens the cross-player leaderboard.
  - ☰ (in the MENU panel) opens Shop, Settings and Character Select.

5. EXTERNAL SERVICES

RevenueCat for in-app purchase infrastructure, and Supabase for the optional leaderboard,
the block list and the report queue. No advertising, analytics, attribution or third-party
data services. The IDFA is not used. Core gameplay needs no network connection.

6. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES

None. Behaviour is identical everywhere. English only, with no region-locked content,
pricing logic or features.

7. REGULATED INDUSTRY OR THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL

Not applicable. All artwork, music, text and code are original and owned by the developer.
No gambling, loot boxes, randomised paid items or wagering.

8. AGE RATING (guideline 2.3.6)

"Messaging and Chat" and "User-Generated Content" are both now set to Yes on the App
Information page. The chat feature is generated on-device and transmits nothing between
players, but the app does present a chat interface and does let players publish a name that
other players see, so Yes is the correct answer to both.

9. PERMISSIONS

The app requests no sensitive permissions: no location, contacts, camera, microphone or
photos, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt. Local notifications are optional and are
scheduled on the device.
