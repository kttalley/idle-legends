===== SHORT DESCRIPTION (80/80) =====
Idle MMORPG with talent trees, offline progress, and a real cross-player ladder.

===== FULL DESCRIPTION (1345/4000) =====
Stillmarch is an idle MMORPG for people who love the shape of an MMO and can't give it forty hours a week.

Pick a class, spend your talent points, and send your hero into the march. Combat resolves itself. You make the decisions that matter: the build, the gear, the route, and which fights are worth picking. Close the app and your hero keeps going. Open it tomorrow and collect.

SKILL TREES WITH TEETH
MMO-style talent trees for every class. Respec any time, and builds play differently instead of just scaling harder.

OFFLINE PROGRESS
Your hero ranges on their own for up to 8 hours, or 16 with Standing Orders. No energy timers, no countdowns nagging you back.

LOOT AND TEMPERING
Craft, temper, and push the quality of the gear you find.

A LIVING WORLD
Zones, gathering nodes, town vendors, and heroes going about their own business around you.

REAL PLAYERS
A cross-player ladder where every rank is another person's hero. Publish your roster, challenge other people's characters, and climb.

SAFE MULTIPLAYER
Report or block any player from their legend page. Reports go to a person who reviews them. You never have to chat with strangers to play.

NO GACHA, NO ADS
No loot boxes, no odds tables, no gems, no ads. Purchases are named things you can see before you buy.

Free to play. Two optional one-time purchases, no subscriptions.
