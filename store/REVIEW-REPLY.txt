GUIDELINE 1.2 - USER-GENERATED CONTENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

All five precautions are now implemented. The user-generated content in this app is the
CHARACTER NAME, which is published to the leaderboard and appears above other players'
characters in the world. Every item below is demonstrated in the attached recording.

1. EULA AGREED BEFORE USE
   A full-screen Terms of Use gate is the first screen on first launch, ahead of character
   selection and ahead of any account being created. It cannot be dismissed; the only way
   past it is the "I agree" button. The first clause on screen, above the fold, reads:

     "There is zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users. Character names
     that contain hate speech, slurs, sexual content, threats, harassment, or that
     impersonate staff are prohibited. Accounts that post them are removed from the
     leaderboard and ejected, without warning and without refund."

   The full terms are at https://design.kristiantalley.com/stillmarch/terms/

2. FILTERING OBJECTIONABLE CONTENT
   Character names are filtered at creation against hate speech, slurs, sexual content,
   harassment terms and staff impersonation. The filter normalises the common evasions
   first - leet substitution, letter spacing, repeated letters and Cyrillic and Greek
   homoglyphs - so that alternate spellings are caught. A rejected name disables the
   "Begin Your Legend" button and explains why. Names are additionally filtered on display,
   so any name published before this build is masked wherever it would be shown.

3. FLAGGING OBJECTIONABLE CONTENT
   Every other player's row on the LADDER carries a flag control. It opens a report dialog
   offering: hate speech or a slur, sexual or explicit content, harassment or threats,
   impersonation, and something else objectionable. Reports are recorded server-side.

4. BLOCKING ABUSIVE USERS
   The same dialog offers "Block and report". Blocking:
     - removes that player from the leaderboard immediately, on the same tap, with no
       refresh required;
     - removes them from the player's world, so their character no longer appears;
     - notifies us, by filing a report automatically alongside the block;
     - persists across reinstalls and devices, as it is stored server-side.

5. ACTING WITHIN 24 HOURS
   We commit to reviewing every report within 24 hours, removing the offending content and
   ejecting the account responsible. Ejection blanks the published name and bars the account
   from publishing again; this is enforced by database policy, not by the client. Our
   internal runbook and the queries that measure compliance are kept with the project.

GUIDELINE 2.1(b) - IN-APP PURCHASE PRODUCTS FAILED TO LOAD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Fixed. The specificity in your report was what led us to the defect, and we are grateful
for it.

The cause was in our code, not in our App Store Connect configuration. Our in-app purchase
module loaded the StoreKit bridge through an asynchronous function that RETURNED the bridge
object itself. JavaScript inspects any value returned this way for a `then` property to
decide whether it is a promise; the bridge forwards every property access to a native
method, so that inspection invoked a native method named `then`, which does not exist. The
call rejected. This happened before configure(), before any product lookup, and before any
purchase, on device only — so every store operation failed identically and silently, and
the paywall could only ever report that no products were available.

We found it by building for the iOS Simulator and reading the native console, where the
error appears as: "Purchases.then() is not implemented on ios". With the bridge returned
inside a wrapper object instead, the same build now configures successfully and receives
both products from StoreKit.

This build additionally:
  - requests the product identifiers directly from StoreKit as well as through our
    provider's configuration, so a provider-side misconfiguration cannot hide the products;
  - states the specific reason on screen whenever a price cannot be loaded, and offers a
    Retry;
  - always shows a product row, even when its price is unavailable, rather than hiding it.

WHERE THE PURCHASES ARE NOW
A SHOP screen has been added as the first item of the menu (the hamburger control),
reachable from anywhere in the game. It lists both products with their prices, and
Restore Purchases, which is reachable without making a purchase first.

We would like to correct our previous note on this point. We stated that Standing Orders
could be reached from the "Looks" panel. That was inaccurate: its only control was inside
the Watch meter, which on iPhone sits within the map view, two levels deep - and it was
hidden entirely whenever a price failed to load. The reviewer was right that there was no
way to reach it. Both products are now on one screen, and a product row is always shown
even when its price is unavailable, rather than being hidden.
