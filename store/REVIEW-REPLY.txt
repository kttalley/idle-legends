3. WHAT THE APP DOES, AND FOR WHOM
Stillmarch is an idle role-playing game. You create a hero, choose a class and spend talent points; combat then resolves automatically while you watch, and continues while the app is closed. The player's decisions are the build, the equipment, which zone to travel to and which fights to take, rather than moment-to-moment input. It is aimed at players who enjoy RPG progression but cannot commit long uninterrupted sessions. The problem it solves is that traditional RPGs demand hours at a time; this one delivers the same progression in short visits.

4. SETTING UP AND REACHING EVERY FEATURE
No credentials are required. No demo account exists because the app has no account system and no login.
- Launch the app, pick a class and race, tap Begin. Play starts immediately.
- Combat runs by itself in the main view. The bottom bar switches between Fight, Mining and Smithing.
- MENU in the bottom dock opens the left panel: character sheet, talent trees, zone travel and The Watch (the offline-progress timer).
- GEAR in the bottom dock opens equipment and inventory.
- CHAT in the bottom dock opens the message feed. These messages are generated locally on the device and are not sent between players.
- LADDER at the top of the left panel opens the cross-player leaderboard.

8. WHAT CAN BE PURCHASED, AND HOW TO REACH IT
Two non-consumable in-app purchases, both entirely optional. Neither is required to complete the game, and neither affects game balance in the player's favour beyond convenience.
- Founder Pack (USD 4.99): three cosmetic colour schemes for the hero. Purely visual, granting no stats, no speed and no change to drop rates.
- Standing Orders (USD 2.99): permanently extends unattended play from 8 hours to 16.
To reach them: tap MENU in the bottom dock, then the "Looks" button at the top of the panel that opens. The Wardrobe shows five colour schemes; three are marked with a padlock and the word FOUNDER. Tapping a locked scheme, or the Unlock button beneath, begins the purchase. Standing Orders appears on the same left panel under the heading "The Watch".
Restore Purchases is at the bottom of the same Wardrobe panel and is reachable without making a purchase first.
